# Refund Policy

**Last updated: 2026-08-01**

Ko-fi processes the payment but does not handle refunds or disputes for Arkitect Pro — that's on us directly. Email **arkitect.pro.app@gmail.com** with your Ko-fi receipt/transaction ID to request one.

## Your right to a refund

**If your license key has not been activated (bound to a Discord account) yet:** we'll refund you in full, no questions asked, within 14 days of purchase.

**If you're an EU/EEA/UK consumer:** you generally have a 14-day right to withdraw from an online purchase without giving a reason. For digital content delivered instantly (like a license key emailed right after purchase), this right can only be lost early if you were explicitly asked, before completing the purchase, to consent to immediate delivery and to acknowledge that doing so waives your withdrawal right. If you weren't clearly asked for that consent at checkout, your 14-day withdrawal right stands regardless of whether you've activated the key — contact us and we'll honor it.

**If your key has already been activated and more than 14 days have passed:** refunds are handled case-by-case (e.g. the app doesn't work on your system and we can't fix it, duplicate purchase, etc.) — email us and we'll work it out.

## What happens on a refund

Your license is deactivated (Pro features re-lock) and, if applicable, your Discord account is unbound so the key can be reissued or retired.

## Chargebacks

If you can get a refund by asking, please ask first — a chargeback costs us processing fees on top of the refund and takes longer to resolve. We're generally easy to reach and quick to respond.
